# Work with me

I'm **Oswaldo Moper**, a senior software engineer focused on **functional
architecture** and **reproducible infrastructure**. I help teams design,
refactor and operate systems that are correct by construction and pleasant to
maintain.

## What I do

- **Haskell & Yesod** — domain modelling, type-driven design, deep refactors
  that preserve semantics while reducing effects and duplication.
- **NixOS & flakes** — reproducible builds, dev environments and CI/CD; turning
  "works on my machine" into "works everywhere, forever".
- **PostgreSQL & data** — schema design, query tuning and safe migrations.
- **Platform / DevOps** — robust pipelines, observability and infrastructure as
  code.
- **Architecture & system design** — alternative designs with explicit
  trade-offs, risk analysis and a clear recommendation.

## How I work

I think in invariants, dependencies and risks. Every change comes with the
reasoning behind it: what it improves, what it could break, and how to verify
it. Purity, composition and clarity — no changing semantics.
